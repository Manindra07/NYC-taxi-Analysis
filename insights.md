### Key findings

1. **Demand is concentrated.** 82% of pickups are in Manhattan; demand peaks at 6 pm, and Friday 5-7 pm is the busiest window. Weekdays follow the commute, weekends run late.
2. **Distance is the fare.** About $4.45 plus $2.81 per mile (R² 0.92). Passenger count has no effect. Short trips cost the most per mile.
3. **Airports have a flat fare.** 126 trips cost exactly $52, and 90% of them touch JFK.
4. **Queens pickups are worth more.** Median fare $21.50 vs $8.50 in Manhattan, because trips are longer, not pricier per mile.
5. **Cash is invisible.** 28% of trips are paid in cash and cash tips are never recorded.
6. **Green cabs show almost no card tips.** 46% of green card trips record no tip vs 5% for yellow, in every distance band. Cause unknown.
7. **Data quality is good.** About 1% of rows were unusable; every money field was complete.
